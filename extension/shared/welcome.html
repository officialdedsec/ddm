Getting started

  1. 1
    Install DDM for Windows

    The add-on hands your downloads to DDM, the program that does the work. Windows 7, 8, 10 and 11 — no administrator rights needed.

  2. 2
    Start DDM once

    It introduces itself to your browsers and waits quietly in the tray, next to the clock.

  3. 3
    Download as usual

    Click any download link, or point at a video and click Download this video above it. Right-click a page for more.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your downloads, history and cookies stay on your PC — the add-on talks only to DDM on this computer, never to a server.

Checks before you open

DDM looks at where a file comes from and what it really is: look-alike and brand-new sites, sites spreading malware, programs disguised as pictures or documents. Then your antivirus scans it.

Fast and resumable

Up to 32 connections per file, and downloads that pick up where they stopped after a lost connection, a restart or an expired link.

Videos in every quality

A Download this video button above the videos you watch, with every quality the site offers — or just the sound as MP3.

Good to know

  • Right-click a link, a picture or a page: Download with DDM, download all links or images, or save the page as PDF.
  • Hold Alt while clicking a link to let the browser download it instead.
  • Click the DDM icon in the toolbar to turn DDM off on one tab.
